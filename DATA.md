# Data policy

The repository contains public hand examples and a provisional, reproducible synthetic source/label fixture freeze with fixed clocks and documentation-range addresses. A separate author produced the generated fixtures; their labels are authored synthetic expectations, not human judgments or measured real-network truth. No heldout quality result is claimed. The hand demo is a mechanics example, not an evaluation cohort.

Do not commit client traffic, real internal addresses, credentials or licence-unverified public samples. Public realism checks, human labels and dataset terms remain separate prerequisites; none is claimed here. Generated truth stays out of detector/model inputs, with dev/heldout split and label identities pinned before evaluation. Expected misses remain positive cases; all eligible benign episodes stay in the benign denominator. Evaluation must bind the actual source, split, labels, seal, parser implementation/runtime, policy/context/clock, method, scorer and adapter. A generator freeze alone does not establish isolation, quality or release acceptance. Do not regenerate or relabel a frozen experiment to repair an unfavorable result.

Normal analysis reads only the caller-selected local source. It does not discover a corpus, open labels or evaluate a split. Optional annotation receives only allowlisted computed numbers/enums, without raw logs, endpoints, UIDs, history, services or labels. `evidence.json` intentionally retains the full original supporting row bytes, including line endings, and reports display endpoint identities; treat these outputs with the input's access restrictions. A hash manifest is not a signature or a grant to retain, publish or access a source. The caller chooses a new output directory, and existing outputs are never overwritten.
